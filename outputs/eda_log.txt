Name: resid, dtype: float64
=> A steady climb of about +3.5% over the last month of every quarter, then a reset on day 1 of the next.
   This is a CALENDAR effect, so it is modelled with a days_to_quarter_end ramp feature.
   Consequence for the December chart: rates should rise through December towards Dec 31.
